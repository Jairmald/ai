# Security Lab Reports

> **"In the world of cybersecurity, knowledge is the best defense."**
> Hands-on labs where theory meets practice — deep dives into enterprise security, threat
> detection, and compliance frameworks.

---

## What Are These?

A collection of **technical lab reports** documenting security implementations, attack detection
workflows, and compliance-driven hardening procedures. Each report walks the full lifecycle —
initial assessment, implementation, validation, and what broke along the way.

These aren't screenshots and notes. They're documentation of the engineering decisions, the
configurations that resulted, and the troubleshooting in between. Every report carries a
*Challenges & Solutions* section, because the parts that didn't work the first time are the parts
worth writing down.

## Why Lab Reports?

- **Portfolio** — technical depth demonstrated rather than claimed
- **Reference** — step-by-step procedures worth returning to
- **Interview preparation** — talking points backed by work actually done
- **Certification study** — methodologies aligned with CISSP, CEH and Security+
- **Team training** — shareable procedures and practices

---

## Report Structure

Every report follows the same shape, so any of them can be skimmed in the same way:

```
1. Lab Title            5. Key Procedures        9.  Deliverables
2. Purpose & Objectives 6. Results               10. Conclusion
3. Frameworks & Tech    7. Challenges & Solutions
4. Tools Used           8. Key Takeaways
```
