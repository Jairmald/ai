I work on the unglamorous half of security: finding what's actually exposed, deciding what
genuinely needs fixing, and cutting the distance between those two. Most recently that meant
automating a vulnerability-to-patch pipeline that turned two days of manual triage into
twelve seconds, and building a dependency scanner that asks whether vulnerable code is ever
actually *called* before it raises an alarm.

I'm drawn to the problems where the hard part isn't detection — it's deciding what deserves
attention. A scanner that flags everything has told you nothing.

Outside of security: shonen manga, the gym, and mentoring people trying to break into the field.
